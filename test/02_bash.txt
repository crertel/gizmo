@@step2

@@end

1. Send the command "uname -a" to the 'bash' mailbox.
2. Issue a receive with dest "output" (the result will appear as ${output}
   on the next eval cycle).
3. Return frames: ["@step2"] to continue to the next step.

Do NOT try to send the result to 'human' in this cycle — ${output} won't have
the bash output until the next cycle.
